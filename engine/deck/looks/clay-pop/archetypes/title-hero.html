Deck mark

Name the thing on the screen first. Say what it is in one plain sentence, then why this room should care about it today.

Point at the model. It is the real object, made in clay so its shape reads at a glance; the pieces floating beside it are its own parts, pulled out so they can be seen.

Frame the talk in three beats: why it matters, how it works, what we found. If asked, say the model is a schematic and which proportions are to scale.
