Deck mark

Walk the numbers, not the words. Say "one", point at the disc marked 1 on the drawing, then read its line. Do the same for two and three. The numbered discs are there so the room never has to search.

Let the explosion do the explaining. When the parts lift apart, say what sits against what. That is the fact people remember, and it is the one a photograph could not show.

Say what is real. Give the real part count and the real proportions, and say which of the two are schematic here. If asked for a dimension you were not given, say it varies and move on.
