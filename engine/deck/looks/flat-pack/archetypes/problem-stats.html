Start with the drawing, not the numbers. Point at the part marked 1 and say what goes wrong there in one sentence. The audience needs the place before they can hold the figures.

Read the stats top to bottom. Each one is the same problem counted a different way - say that out loud, or they will hear three problems. Give the source of every number as you say it.
