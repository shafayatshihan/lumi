Deck mark

Open on the object. Name the thing on the page in one plain sentence before you say anything about the theory. The drawing is doing the introducing; let it.

Point at the parts. Say how many there are and which one the talk is really about - that is the blue one. Say that the parts lift apart the way they do in an assembly manual, so nothing is hidden.

Frame the talk in three beats: why it matters, how it works, what we found. If asked whether the drawing is to scale, say which proportions are real and which are schematic - do not guess.
