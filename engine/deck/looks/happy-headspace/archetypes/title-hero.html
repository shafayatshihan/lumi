Deck mark

Start slowly. This look gives you a beat before the first word lands; use it. Say what the thing is in one unhurried sentence, then stop and let the room catch up.

Point at the orange form. There is one orange shape in the picture and it is the subject. Name it, then say why it matters to the people in front of you today.

Frame the talk in three beats: why it matters, how it works, what we found. If asked whether the shapes are to scale, say which proportions are real and which are a simplification - do not guess.
