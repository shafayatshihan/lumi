Deck mark

Walk the numbers, not the words. Say "one", point at the disc marked 1, then read its line. Do the same for two and three. The discs are there so the room never has to search.

Let the drift do the explaining. The parts ease apart and back on one slow breath; while they are apart, say what sits against what. That is the fact people remember.

Say what is real. Give the real part count and the real proportions, and say which of the two are simplified here. If asked for a dimension you were not given, say it varies and move on.
