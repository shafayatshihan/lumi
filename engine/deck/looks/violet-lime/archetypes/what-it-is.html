The number the talk turns on

Deck mark

Start with the whole object, then its parts. Say what it is in one sentence a first-year would follow, then name the parts on the right in the order the eye reads them.

Say where the pictures come from. If these are photographs, say who took them and when. If they are plates carrying numbers rather than pictures, say the numbers out loud and give their source - a plate is on the slide because the figure matters more than a picture would, not because a picture was missing.

Land on the lime pill. It is the one number the rest of the talk depends on; say it, say where it came from, and say what it would take to change it. Honesty caveat: name which dimensions are measured and which are nominal. Prepare the question "how do you know it is that size?".
