Deck mark

Follow the tour, do not lead it. The camera visits one part per six seconds and the matching card turns lime; say each part's name as the card lights, then what it does in one sentence.

Give the real quantities at each stop - what goes in, what comes out, at what rate - and say where each figure came from. If a rate is nominal rather than measured, say "nominal" out loud.

Close by naming the part that limits the whole. Every system has one, and the room will remember it. Honesty caveat: say which parts are drawn to scale relative to each other and which are enlarged to be visible. Prepare the question "what fails first?".
