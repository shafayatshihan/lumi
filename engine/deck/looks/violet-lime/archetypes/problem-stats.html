Deck mark

Walk the blocks left to right, and spend twice as long on the lime one as on the other two together. The colour has already told the room which one matters; your voice should agree with it.

Give each number its source as you say it. If a figure came from the user's own data, say whose and when it was measured; if it is published, name the paper. Any number you are not sure of comes off the slide rather than onto it with a hedge.

Close on the common cause. That sentence is the hinge into the rest of the talk - the three problems are one problem, and the next slides are about that one. Prepare the question "are these really the same thing?", and answer it with the mechanism, not with the numbers again.
