Deck mark

Read the lime dial first and say what it is a fraction of - a bare percentage means nothing until the room knows the denominator. Then the other two, quickly; they are context for the first.

Give the source and the spread. Say how many runs, over what period, and how much the number moved between the best and the worst. A dial shows a single figure, so the variation has to come from you.

Then say what the number means for the decision this talk is asking the room to make. Honesty caveat: if any figure is modelled rather than measured, say so as you read it. Prepare the question "what would make that number worse?", and answer with the condition, not the caveat.
