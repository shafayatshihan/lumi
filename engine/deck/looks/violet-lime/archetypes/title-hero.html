Deck mark

Name the thing on the screen first. Say what it is in one plain sentence, then why this room should care about it today. Do not read the title back to them; they have already read it.

Point at the picture. Say what it shows and where it came from - your own photograph of the real thing, or a drawing of it built from real dimensions. Say which proportions are to scale and which are schematic, so nobody measures the picture later.

Then set the clock. Tell them how long you will take and what they will be able to do or decide by the end. Say the one thing this slide must leave behind, in a single sentence, and move on.
