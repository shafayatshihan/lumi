Deck mark

Let the picture reach a step before you name it. The cards light in time with the figure; talk at the pace of the loop rather than ahead of it, and the room will follow the object instead of your words.

Say what moves and what stays still at each step, and give the real rate - how long the step takes in the real world, not how long the animation takes. State the loop as a schematic where it is one.

Finish on what has changed between the first card and the last: that difference is the whole point of the process. Honesty caveat: name any step compressed or idealised for the picture. Prepare the question "what happens when it goes wrong at step two?".
