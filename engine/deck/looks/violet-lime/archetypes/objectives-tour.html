Then the goals, in order, and do not skip the last one. A checklist with one cross on it is more credible than four ticks, so say plainly what was not delivered and why - a reason, not an excuse.

Say what happens next for the unfinished one, with a date you can defend. Honesty caveat: if a goal was redefined partway through, say so here rather than quietly counting it as delivered. Prepare the question "what changed between the plan and now?".
