Deck mark

Open by naming the object on screen, not the theory behind it. Say what it is in one plain sentence, then say why this room should care about it today.

Point at the model: tell them it is built from the real geometry and that the motion they see is the behaviour this talk explains.

Frame the talk in three beats: why it matters, how it works, what we found. If asked about the model, say it is a schematic render, not a photograph.
