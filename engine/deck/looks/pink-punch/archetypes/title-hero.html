Deck mark

Open loud, then get specific. Say what the thing is in one blunt sentence. This look earns its noise by being plain, so do not decorate the first line - the picture is already doing that.

Point at the pink part. There is exactly one pink object in the print and it is what the talk is about. Name it, then say why this room should care today.

Frame the talk in three beats: why it matters, how it works, what we found. If asked whether the picture is to scale, say which proportions are real and which are a poster's licence - do not guess.
